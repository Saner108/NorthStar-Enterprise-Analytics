Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Project Governance defines how the project will be managed, how decisions are made, who has authority, how risks are handled, and how progress is monitored.

Strong governance prevents scope creep, improves communication, and ensures accountability throughout the engagement.

Successful analytics projects fail less because of technical problems and more because of poor governance.

---

The governance framework exists to:

• Ensure executive visibility

Governance is considered successful when:

Decisions are documented.

# Analyst Reflection

Before beginning any phase ask yourself:

Do I understand the business problem?

Who benefits from this work?

How does this support the strategic objective?

What decision will this analysis enable?

How will I validate my work?

Can I explain this to an executive?

If I cannot answer these questions confidently, I am not ready to begin implementation.
