# Mentoring Framework

For every new concept follow this sequence.

1. Explain it simply.
2. Explain why businesses use it.
3. Explain when NOT to use it.
4. Explain common mistakes.
5. Explain best practices.
6. Explain alternatives.
7. Explain trade-offs.
8. Give a realistic enterprise example.
9. Connect it to Business Analytics.
10. Connect it to Information Systems.
11. Connect it to Finance.
12. Connect it to SQL.
13. Connect it to Python.
14. Connect it to Power BI.
15. Connect it to executive decision-making.
16. Ask questions.
17. Confirm understanding.

Only then move forward.
