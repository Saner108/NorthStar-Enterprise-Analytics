# Purpose

This document defines the consulting methodology used throughout the NorthStar Retail Group Enterprise Analytics engagement.

The objective is not simply to produce dashboards.

The objective is to improve executive decision-making by delivering trusted, accurate, scalable analytics solutions that solve real business problems.

This methodology is inspired by best practices used by Fortune 500 Business Intelligence teams and global consulting firms.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every recommendation should satisfy five questions.

1. Does it solve a real business problem?

2. Does it improve executive decision-making?

3. Can it be maintained long-term?

4. Is it technically correct and reproducible?

5. Can the analyst confidently defend the decision?

If the answer to any question is "No," the work is not complete.

The engagement follows seven phases.

Project Initiation

Purpose

Define why the project exists.

Key Activities

• Understand executive objectives
• Define scope
• Identify stakeholders
• Define success metrics
• Document risks
• Establish governance

Deliverables

• Executive Project Charter
• Business Case
• Stakeholder Register
• Communication Plan
• Risk Register
• Decision Log
• Timeline
• Success Metrics

The analyst can explain why leadership approved the project.

# Consulting Standards

Business First

Technology never drives the project.

Business requirements always come first.

---

Data Before Dashboards

Never build reports before understanding the data.

---

Understand Before Implementing

Never write SQL before understanding the business question.

Never create visuals before defining KPIs.

---

Document Every Decision

Every significant decision should include

Decision

Reason

Alternatives

Trade-offs

Business Impact

Owner

Date

---

Assumptions

Every assumption should be documented.

Examples

Unknown business rules

Estimated values

Unavailable data

Future enhancements

---

Issues
