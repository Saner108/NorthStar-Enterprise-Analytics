# Executive Summary

NorthStar Retail Group (NRG) is a rapidly growing omnichannel general merchandise retailer operating approximately 250 retail stores across the United States with an expanding e-commerce platform.

Founded in 2004, NorthStar has grown through organic expansion and multiple regional acquisitions. While this growth has increased revenue and market presence, it has also created significant operational complexity.

The company's technology infrastructure has not evolved at the same pace as the business.

Departments frequently operate with separate reporting processes, inconsistent KPI definitions, and disconnected data sources. Executive leadership has identified reporting modernization as one of the organization's highest strategic priorities for the upcoming fiscal year.

The Enterprise Operational Excellence Initiative was approved to improve reporting accuracy, operational visibility, data quality, and executive decision-making across the organization.

# Why This Consulting Engagement Exists

The executive leadership team recognizes that future growth depends on trustworthy data.

As the organization expands, manually producing reports is no longer sustainable.

Leaders require a single source of truth to make timely operational and financial decisions.

The Business Intelligence department has been tasked with leading an enterprise reporting transformation that establishes standardized metrics, improves data quality, and enables scalable analytics.

This consulting engagement represents the first phase of that transformation.
